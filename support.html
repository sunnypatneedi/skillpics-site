Ace Support

Ace helps you learn from saved screenshots, privately on your iPhone.

Getting started

  1. Choose a topic, like context or leadership. You can open See an example first.
  2. Tap Choose screenshots and select 1–5 images with readable text. Ace prepares a guide automatically.
  3. Read the ideas grouped by subject. Expand Source to check the supporting text and saved preview.
  4. Use Guide options to change your topic, manage saved screenshots, or open privacy information.

Requirements

Guide generation requires iOS 26 or later, an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone, and an available on-device model. Enable Apple Intelligence in Settings and allow its model to download. Availability depends on language and region. The built-in example works without the model.

Common questions

No matching ideas appeared. Try a topic present in your selected screenshots and use images with clear text. This version does not fully understand text-free photos or visual diagrams.

A screenshot could not be prepared. Its text may be unreadable, its image unavailable, or the on-device model unable to produce a supported explanation. Try another image. Completed ideas remain saved.

An explanation is wrong. Check its Source, then expand Edit this explanation to correct it or remove the lesson. AI explanations and text recognition can be wrong.

How do I remove saved data? Open Saved screenshots from Guide options or About Ace and swipe to delete a source. Its saved text, preview, and lessons are removed from Ace. Original Photos images remain unchanged.

Does Ace upload my screenshots? No cloud AI processes your images or text. Apple's Photos picker may download an original already in your iCloud library. See our privacy policy.

Does it connect to LinkedIn, X, or Instagram? No. Save a screenshot yourself, then select it in Ace. The app does not automatically monitor social services.

Where is the previous library interface? The current iPhone experience focuses on topic guides. Existing captures remain stored, and readable saved sources can be prepared from a guide. The native Mac preview retains its topic-wiki interface.
