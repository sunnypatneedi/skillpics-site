SkillPics Support

SkillPics turns a screenshot of a tip, framework, or prompt into a reusable AI skill — entirely on your device.

Getting started

  1. Open SkillPics and tap Pick a Screenshot, or tap Auto-Scan to find skills across your screenshots.
  2. Review the skill, switch the format, and tap Open in Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini — the skill is copied so you can paste it into a new chat.
  3. Your skills collect in the Library, where you can search, filter, edit, archive, or delete them.

Requirements

Making skills uses Apple Intelligence, available on iPhone 15 Pro and newer with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri).

Common questions

Auto-Scan found nothing. Make sure SkillPics has Full Photos access (Settings → SkillPics → Photos → Full Access).

The AI app opened but my skill wasn't filled in. iOS doesn't let apps type into each other — your skill is on the clipboard, so tap the chat box and Paste.
