Ace Support

Ace helps you learn from saved screenshots, privately on your iPhone or Mac.

Getting started

  1. Tap Connect Photos and allow access once. You can open See an example first.
  2. Ace discovers screenshots automatically, newest first. Ideas appear as local preparation finishes. Explore them or search a topic, like context or leadership.
  3. Start with one useful idea and its source-backed steps, when present. Tap View screenshot to check the evidence.
  4. Tap Learn this to copy a prepared prompt and open ChatGPT or Claude. Paste it yourself to begin. Use the destination selector to switch apps. Idea options contains prompt preview/editing, Apply instead, other ideas, and saved-source management.

Preparation runs while Ace is active, pauses when inactive, and resumes when you return. Limited Photos access restricts coverage. This release automatically discovers assets Photos classifies as screenshots; it does not scan every camera photo. You can pause discovery in About Ace.

Requirements

Guide generation requires iOS 26 or macOS 26 or later, an Apple Intelligence-capable device, and an available on-device model. Enable Apple Intelligence in Settings and allow its model to download. Availability depends on language and region. The built-in example works without the model.

Common questions

No matching ideas appeared. Preparation may still be working. Search a topic from your library or explore the prepared ideas. This version does not fully understand text-free photos or visual diagrams.

A screenshot could not be prepared. Its text may be unreadable, its image unavailable, or the on-device model unable to produce a supported explanation. Ace retries temporary failures on a later pass. Completed ideas remain saved.

An explanation is wrong. Open Idea options → All ideas & editing, then expand Edit this explanation to correct it or remove the lesson. AI explanations and text recognition can be wrong.

How do I remove saved data? Open Saved screenshots from Idea options or About Ace and swipe to delete a source. Its saved text, preview, and lessons are removed from Ace. Original Photos images remain unchanged.

Does Ace upload my screenshots? No cloud AI processes your images or text. Automatic discovery skips cloud-only originals. See our privacy policy.

Does it connect to LinkedIn, X, or Instagram? No. Save a screenshot normally; Ace discovers it from Photos while active. The app does not automatically monitor social services.

Where is the previous library interface? The current iPhone experience focuses on topic guides. Existing captures remain stored, and readable saved sources can be prepared from a guide. The native Mac app uses the same idea-first experience. iPhone and Mac libraries are currently separate; Ace does not sync them.
