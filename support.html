Ace Support

Ace organizes saved screenshots into a private library of places, books, recipes, products, contacts, and useful ideas, on your iPhone.

Getting started

  1. Finish onboarding and tap Pick a Screenshot, or start Auto-Scan to process screenshots Ace can access.
  2. Open a saved item to review its details and available actions. Open See All to browse and search the Library.
  3. Use the chat button for Chat with Ace. Open Menu → Patterns to view saving patterns.
  4. For reusable AI skills, choose an export format and copy or open it in another app. You choose what to share.

Requirements

Ace requires iOS 26. AI classification, chat, and generation need a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled, downloaded, and available in your language and region. If the model is unavailable, Ace explains the limitation.

Common questions

Auto-Scan found nothing. Check Photos access in Settings → Apps → Ace → Photos. Limited access allows only selected images. Check that your scan scope contains screenshots with readable text. You can also pick an image individually or share it from Photos.

A detail or suggested intent is wrong. Review the original screenshot and use the item's edit or feedback controls. OCR and AI can make mistakes.

The other AI app opened but my text was not filled in. Tap the chat box and Paste. Ace does not submit messages on your behalf.

Does Ace upload my screenshots? The App Store build processes content on your device and has no cloud helper enabled. External links and content you choose to share are handled by the destination app or website. See our privacy policy.

When does Auto-Scan run? You start scanning in the app. Ace does not promise unattended overnight scanning.
