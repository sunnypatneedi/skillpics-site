Ace Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Ace is built to be private. Your screenshots and the library Ace builds from them are processed on your device and stay there.

What we collect

Nothing. Ace has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not have servers that receive your photos or skills.

Photos and generated guides

After you connect Photos, Ace automatically discovers screenshots available under your Photos permission. Full access enables library-wide discovery; limited access restricts what Ace can find. Automatic discovery reads local originals and does not download cloud-only images. Ace uses Vision text recognition and Apple's on-device model locally; it does not upload your images or recognized text to an AI service.

Ace stores recognized text, a source preview, and generated explanations on your device. You can edit or remove explanations, or delete a saved source from Saved screenshots. Deleting it removes its saved text, preview, and lessons from Ace; original photos remain unchanged. Ace stores a local progress ledger so preparation can resume and sources you remove are not automatically reimported. You can pause automatic discovery in About Ace. Preparation runs while the app is active and resumes when you return.

Optional cloud helper

Some iPhones without Apple Intelligence can use an optional cloud helper. If — and only if — that option is enabled, Ace reads your screenshot's text on device and sends just that text (never the image) to the helper to craft a skill. The current App Store build does not enable any cloud helper; everything runs on device.

Learning in another AI app

When you tap “Learn this,” Ace prepares a prompt on-device from the selected idea and its supporting quote, copies it to the local clipboard, and opens ChatGPT or Claude (or its website). The navigation URL contains no screenshot text. Ace does not automatically paste or submit a message. You can preview or edit the prompt first. When you choose to paste and send, the destination's privacy policy applies.

The handoff disables Universal Clipboard transfer. Other local apps with clipboard access may still read copied content. iPhone clipboard exports expire after ten minutes. iPhone and Mac libraries are separate; this release adds no cloud synchronization.

Children

Ace is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
