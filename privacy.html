Ace Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Ace is built to be private. Your screenshots and the library Ace builds from them are processed on your device and stay there.

What we collect

Nothing. Ace has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not have servers that receive your photos or skills.

Photos

When you pick a screenshot or run Auto-Scan, Ace reads the image locally using Apple's on-device intelligence and Vision text recognition. Your photos are never uploaded, copied off your device, or shared with us or anyone else. The skills you create are stored only on your device.

Optional cloud helper

Some iPhones without Apple Intelligence can use an optional cloud helper. If — and only if — that option is enabled, Ace reads your screenshot's text on device and sends just that text (never the image) to the helper to craft a skill. The current App Store build does not enable any cloud helper; everything runs on device.

Sharing to other apps

When you tap “Open in Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini,” Ace copies the skill text to your clipboard and opens that app or website. From that point, the other app's own privacy policy applies. Ace does not send anything on its own.

Children

Ace is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
